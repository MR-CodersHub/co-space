Success Stories: How Startups Thrive at CoSpace

Discover proven strategies to get the most out of your coworking membership and boost your daily productivity.

Sarah Jenkins

Community Manager & Productivity Coach

1. Attend Community Events

Most coworking spaces host a variety of events—from casual Friday happy hours to intensive skill-building workshops. These aren't just perks; they are prime opportunities to network, learn something new, and find potential collaborators or clients. Make it a goal to attend at least one event per week.

2. Utilize Different Zones for Different Tasks

A well-designed coworking space offers diverse environments. Use the vibrant, open-plan areas for brainstorming and collaborative work, then retreat to a quiet zone or a focus pod when you need to do deep, uninterrupted work. Changing your physical environment can reset your focus and spark creativity.

"The beauty of a coworking space is flexibility. Don't chain yourself to one desk. Let your environment dictate your workflow."

3. Introduce Yourself

It sounds simple, but initiating a conversation at the coffee machine can lead to incredible opportunities. Don't be afraid to ask people what they do. The community manager is also a great resource—tell them about your business, and they can often connect you with members who have complementary skills.

Casual conversations often spark the best ideas.

4. Take Real Breaks

When working from home, the line between work and relaxation blurs. Use the structured environment of a coworking space to enforce healthy habits. Step away from your laptop, grab a premium coffee, and read a book in the lounge area. Your brain needs downtime to function optimally.

5. Maximize the Amenities

  • Meeting Rooms: Impress clients by hosting them in professional, fully-equipped conference rooms instead of noisy cafes.
  • Phone Booths: Use soundproof booths for sensitive calls to maintain confidentiality and avoid disturbing others.
  • Wellness Facilities: If your space has a gym or meditation room, integrate it into your daily routine.

Conclusion

A coworking membership is an investment in your career and well-being. By actively engaging with the community and intentionally utilizing the physical space, you can transform it from merely a place to work into a catalyst for your professional growth.
