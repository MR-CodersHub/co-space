Privacy Policy

Your privacy is crucial to us. Learn how we handle and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: January 01, 2026

Table of Contents

1. Information We Collect

To run our coworking platform and spaces effectively, we collect details from you directly and automatically:

  • Personal Details: Name, professional email address, phone contact, billing cards, and official identity documents for access credentials.
  • Device Details: Smart WiFi login credentials, portal telemetry, IP addresses, browser layouts, and usage timestamps.
  • Lobby & Physical Space Details: Secure entry/exit RFID timestamps, along with safety camera footage recorded inside standard public corridors.

2. How We Use Information

Your details are processed to optimize workspace productivity and platform security:

  • Facilitating secure physical space entrance, booking desks, and meeting rooms.
  • Managing financial invoicing records and billing authorization transfers.
  • Maintaining high safety inside standard coworking facilities via corridor cameras and keycard sensors.

3. Cookies and Tracking

Our website utilizes secure technical cookies and pixels to remember active interface preferences (e.g. keeping your active Light or Dark mode active) and analyze anonymous workspace visitor telemetry to speed up system operations.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not lease or sell member information. We only share details with trusted technical partners to authorize card payments, provide secure smart card locks, or when legally compelled by law enforcement agencies.

5. Storage and Security

CoSpace employs premium enterprise firewalls, 256-bit SSL encryption, database tokenization, and physical access controls to protect member archives. However, no transmission method over the public web is 100% immune from rare grid interruptions.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on local regulations, you possess rights to review personal data archived in our portal, correct outdated directories, or request complete removal of your member profile. Contact us directly at privacy@cospace.com to submit standard data requests.
